# Bounded cross-session reconciliation

Status: accepted

## Contract

After typed extraction, before its immutable stage commits, the server discovers
current exact assertion families across sessions in the same Brain. Normalize
subject/predicate with the existing assertion-key function; require exact kind,
selection, manifest and fact validity. Inspect at most the canonical 500 family
members. Only current, supported, retained, active, unreviewed machine-maintained
revisions without rejection/withdrawal fences are eligible. Human-owned/reviewed
alternatives are never targets. Similarity, recency and the newest timestamp do
not select a target. Discovery does not call a model.

For a candidate with no extractor-selected target, exactly one eligible family
member with a different value may become a server-offered replacement hypothesis.
Freeze that revision in the run/input links and normalized stage before assessment.
The existing independent assessor must support both the complete new assertion
and explicit change of that exact old assertion. A hypothesis is not permission
to publish: omission, repetition, historical quotations, assistant plans and
unsupported change preserve the old current head. The extractor cannot nominate
an identity it was not offered. Retirements retain the existing exact offered-ID
and independently supported obsolescence rule; fresh-session source assertions
can discover replacement hypotheses without claiming a universal retirement parser.

An identical sole eligible family/value is a reuse hypothesis. After independent
whole-assertion support succeeds and the frozen original is rechecked, reuse its
identity without a duplicate or fabricated corroboration revision. Preserve the
new evidence and assessment in their own run/stage. A reuse adds no independent
corroboration weight and does not extend old evidence retention.

Offer at most twelve revisions in total, including earlier lineage candidates,
and only whole canonical views fitting the remaining input budget. Multiple
eligible family matches or insufficient room record bounded native discovery
coverage, omit the target and preserve alternatives; never pick an arbitrary
victim or truncate required inputs. Keep the candidate's ordinary conflict/rule
assessment. Discovery metadata is derived controlled stage content and inherits
stage expiry/erasure/replay. Frozen targets cannot expand during retry.

## Acceptance

Prove a new-session explicit correction appends to the same identity after support;
unsupported correction leaves the old head; identical supported repetition reuses
without duplicates; unrelated environment/manifest/history and reviewed/rejected
alternatives are preserved; ambiguous or over-budget target sets select no victim.
Saved-stage restart/replacement uses its frozen targets with no extra discovery
or charges. Source/target erasure removes derived metadata through existing closure.

## Explicit Deferrals

Local acceptance and the versioned synthetic support comparison are recorded in
[the implementation mapping](../mappings/memory-source-support-staging-2026-10-07.md).
Deployment remains separate from the archived execution pack. The evaluation does
not establish general semantic accuracy or every typed producer-action benchmark. No additional runtime, hidden provider, automatic command execution or
change to human authority is authorized by this contract.
