# Source support before usable memory

Status: accepted

## Source

The user's October 7 implement-all instruction accepts the source-support phase
of the [automatic memory plan](../research/automated-memory-improvement-plan-2026-10-07.md).
This amendment strengthens [autonomous maintenance](memory-autonomous-maintenance.md),
[provider learning](memory-provider-policy-and-learning.md),
[claims](memory-claims-and-time.md) and [handovers](memory-procedures-and-handovers.md).
It replaces citation-bounds-only admission with whole-assertion support assessment.
It does not grant operational verification or execution permission.

## Contract

### Assessment and usable knowledge

Every unreviewed canonical revision, including device/browser contributions, needs
a current versioned `supported` assessment before entering any model-facing
investigation, strict recall, synthesis, answering, ranking, embedding or graph
projection. `insufficient`, `contradicted`, unchecked and unavailable outcomes stay
inspectable as history with their disposition. Valid quotes are necessary evidence,
not proof of meaning. Check the complete assertion, rationale, fact time, entities,
environment and source role; procedures include every condition, step and outcome.
Replacements additionally need explicit evidence for changing the offered target;
retirement needs explicit evidence of obsolescence. Omission, repetition, similarity,
assistant proposals and newer timestamps do not establish a correction.

Explicit human authority is a server-authenticated browser review decision with
matching reviewer and canonical decision identity. Transport origin alone is never
human review. The exception preserves existing evidence, review-rule, scope,
retention and operational constraints. Each handover contributor independently
passes the same gate even when the parent has human review. Auditing does not
rewrite author-owned text and is separate from permission to maintain it.
An explicitly reviewed independently retained claim preserves qualified
investigation/history after its source bytes expire, as the existing retrieval
and retention contracts require; strict modes still need available support.
This does not admit expired source bytes, unreviewed expired-support memory,
erased/fenced input or an unsupported required contributor. Already-expired
reviewed source metadata does not impose the source's old deadline on the claim;
still-live support deadlines continue to invalidate delivery and cached context.

### Durable typed stages

Learning validates all extraction fields, spans, offered target identities and
scope first. It then commits one bounded typed stage per learning run, containing
at most eight normalized ClaimContent candidates and twelve retirements with exact
target revisions. Maximum serialized stage is 65,536 bytes. It stores no raw
provider response, source transcript or arbitrary caller prompt. Extraction request,
assessment operation, policy, prompt/schema/verifier versions and exact canonical
dependencies are retained separately. A stage is immutable except for its bounded
verdict, disposition and privacy removal. No canonical replacement or retirement
occurs before a supported verdict. A mixed batch may publish supported members;
unsupported members cannot displace existing knowledge.

The independent assessment uses the installed model through the shared gateway,
the extraction purpose and original permitted source classes plus claim permission.
Only the internal versioned support prompt may resolve a typed learning stage or
an unchecked revision for verification. It receives canonical provenance and
exact permitted evidence, never gold labels or an ordinary usable-claim bypass.
Structured output contains exactly one indexed disposition per offered action,
`supported`, `insufficient` or `contradicted`, and a bounded plain reason. Validate
the full index set, duplicate/missing indices and fields before persisting verdicts.
The versioned assessor separately declares citation support and returns a short
verbatim evidence quotation. A positive disposition also requires that quotation
to occur inside the action's exact cited spans or an explicitly admitted canonical
contributor/fact. Framing windows, the candidate and manifest applicability cannot
provide the quotation. Absent or misattributed quotations normalize to terminal
insufficient evidence, without another paid retry. This anchors the model's
judgment; it does not make a matching quotation proof of semantic correctness.
Empty stages complete without another model call.

Generated handovers use an analogous bounded immutable typed stage before any
canonical append. Freeze normalized complete ClaimContent, exact run/input/base/
policy/write-operation identities and the synthesis receipt; use a separate
assessment operation and commit its typed verdict before publication. The assessor
checks every summary, completed item, next step and risk against original evidence
and exact supported contributors, preserving proposals and observations. Supported
publication atomically appends the revision and its canonical support assessment.
An unsupported new handover allocates no identity; an unsupported refresh preserves
the prior head. Load saved stages/verdicts before making a call. Assessment-only
replacements inherit the draft and original lifetime, never repeat synthesis.
Handover stages follow the same input, privacy, replay, no-call admission and
bounded local recovery rules as learning stages; they are not fake learning runs
or prematurely canonical claims.
Storage freezes run, input, policy, actor/device, write-operation and retry
identities. Recovery counters advance monotonically only through a bounded failed
to queued transition. Operational status can change; only canonical privacy removal
may scrub scope, and canonical audit expiry may clear the title. Removed work cannot
resume. A supported refresh blocked by a review rule or unresolved conflict cannot
replace its prior head.

Assessment source inputs contain the complete cited spans with two lines of
surrounding context, merging overlaps and retaining original one-based line
coordinates. Preserve the first and last eight lines and Markdown headings with
adjacent context independently of extractor-selected citations. The representation
separately identifies exact `cited_spans`; surrounding windows supply qualification
and contradiction checks, never positive support for an unrelated citation. It
reports omitted lines; it never represents a
partial transcript as complete. Original source identities, provenance, content
classes, fences and retention remain authoritative. Required spans and assertion
fields are never silently truncated. The shared aggregate input-byte limit still
applies to source windows, actions and offered revisions together. An oversized
required span remains unavailable; unrelated transcript padding alone does not
require a larger Brain budget or a user-selected excerpt.

### Recovery, automation and privacy

Extraction and assessment use separate durable operation keys and accounting.
Restart after stage commit resumes assessment; restart after verdict commit resumes
publication, rechecking policy, current base revisions, rejection rules, exact
inputs, deadlines and lease. A completed request whose typed result was lost before
commit is result-unavailable; an unknown external outcome is uncertain. Neither is
blindly replayed. Known completed permitted failures may have at most two separately
charged replacements at five/thirty minutes. Semantic insufficiency is terminal for
unchanged inputs. Pre-transmission budget/concurrency denial defers the same work
without consuming that bound; UTC allowance reset automatically resumes admission.

A known completed assessment failure replaces only that assessment: the linked
attempt inherits the exact immutable candidate batch, extraction receipt, offered
revisions and original stage timestamp. It receives a new separately charged
assessment key; it cannot rediscover targets, extend retention or repeat extraction.
If extraction itself failed before a stage existed, its bounded replacement may
extract again. Transient database/publication failures use the existing bounded
worker recovery on the same generation and resume committed stages/verdicts.
Neither recovery mechanism authorizes replay of an uncertain or lost provider
response. Terminal semantic dispositions never schedule another assessment.

Legacy and direct unreviewed revisions are audited in bounded automatic passes under
the standing Brain policy writer. Persist a verdict tied to exact revision and
verifier/policy versions; absence never means supported. Current access, scope,
support availability and child dependencies remain checked on every delivery.
Changing input/policy creates a new assessment generation, not a duplicate of an
unchanged completed generation. Human rejection and erasure always win races.

Cutover invalidates existing cached answers atomically before the first audit.
Delivery checks the recursive closure of exact contributors and their canonical
current heads, including scope, review rules, source/fact lifetimes and both
manifest pointers and supports. Every selected non-null manifest snapshot must
exist in the Brain and remain logically active. Delivery deadlines use the same
closure, including repository snapshot time rather than manifest creation time;
logical expiry invalidates cached context before background cleanup runs.

Audit backfill reaches historical revisions required by current roots recursively.
Assessment prerequisites use the exact immutable contribution lineage. Changed
current heads are recorded as delivery/privacy dependencies, never substituted
as corroborating evidence for an old assertion. They do not make a lineage audit
wait for a current revision that itself depends on that lineage. Final delivery
still requires every exact and current dependency to pass. No cyclic exact
contribution graph may supply usable knowledge.

### Bounded audit discovery — 2026-10-09

Prepare legacy audit candidates in an authenticated repeatable-read, read-only
transaction outside Brain admission locks. Examine at most 16 missing immutable
revision identities per pass, start no further qualification after four eligible
candidates or two seconds, and limit each complete qualification statement to
one second. An in-flight statement may finish within that final second. A timed-out
candidate remains unresolved and is revisited on a later walk; it is never
recorded as unsupported, assessed or permanently excluded. Full target closure,
exact prerequisites, cycle, review, privacy and retention checks remain intact.

Persist only a UUID keyset boundary and monotonic cursor version, scoped by
Brain, exact policy generation and verifier version. Walk every missing revision,
including historical contributors; wrap after the end. Unavailable early rows
and process restarts cannot permanently hide later candidates. The boundary is
scheduling metadata, carries no source text or verdict, has no authority to make
memory supported, and participates in canonical Brain deletion.

Publish through a fresh writer transaction: check enabled actor, current Brain
writer role/archive state, unchanged standing policy ownership/enablement and
exact cursor version. Requalify at most four prepared candidates against complete
current canonical gates, using a 750 ms statement limit and starting no further
qualification after a one-second pass budget. No stale prepared eligibility
authorizes enqueue. A changed policy or cursor discards preparation; other
canonical changes are handled by complete
fresh qualification. Concurrent progress cannot be overwritten. Commit cursor
progress and existing audit/retry admission independently from other maintenance
so an unrelated later failure cannot repeatedly undo discovery progress.

Discovery and timeout handling make no model calls. Keep the existing four-job
admission bound, capacity, budget, recorded-attempt exclusions and bounded native/
provider retry rules. Discovery limits bound examined identities, never truncate
an identity's dependency closure or reinterpret a timeout as a negative verdict.

Audit database/lease exhaustion may resume the same operation at most twice,
after five/thirty minutes, only with no admitted request or a committed typed
verdict. Preserve cumulative worker attempts and permit at most three native
attempts per recovery round. A recorded successful, running, uncertain or
suppressed request without its typed verdict cannot authorize resend. The exact
worker job/token and frozen policy generation are checked before admission and
after the response; a successor's live lease never authorizes an old worker.

Stages/verdict reasons and input links are governed derived content. RLS uses Brain
reader/writer authority and exact composite foreign keys. Source/target expiry or
erasure suppresses staged work and removes its payload and reasons. Durable privacy
journals include stage/verdict identities and replay the same dependency closure
into older backups. Request metadata remains subject to audit retention; no secret
or provider body is stored. New tables must participate in Brain deletion.
Stage-only expiry removes its draft and verdict reason, while a separately
published canonical revision and assessment follow their own lifetime and original
evidence dependencies. It does not extend either lifetime or erase independently
retained knowledge. Source/contributor erasure still removes every dependent
stage, canonical assessment and revision, including in older backups.

Handover inspection exposes only bounded stage state, disposition/reason while
logically active, verifier version and safe assessment receipt/state/error. A
malformed completed response may expose its durable receipt without a disposition;
it never appears supported or grants retry permission. Manifest applicability sent
to assessors contains exact selected repository entries with omitted coverage.
Explicit whole-manifest support remains whole evidence under its existing permit.

## Acceptance

Use the frozen [support baseline](operations-integrated-evaluations.md#automatic-memory-support-baseline-2026-10-07)
plus production tests for procedures, offered targets, handover dependencies, direct
contributions, protected human controls and every consumer. Prove supported positives
survive, false assertions and unsupported corrections are withheld, and recovery
after each commit boundary avoids replay charges. Cover stage-two budget deferral,
concurrent base changes, expiry, erasure, journal replay and automatic legacy audit.
Mocked verdicts prove plumbing only; record the separately governed synthetic
real-model assessment before claiming semantic quality or deployment readiness.

## Explicit Deferrals

Automatic digests/minimal excerpts, compact scoped briefs and cross-session target
discovery remain separate phases of the full authorized plan. They may not bypass
this support guard. Universal truth checking and autonomous command execution are
outside this contract.
