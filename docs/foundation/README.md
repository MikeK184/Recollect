These are accepted product requirements, not a progress report. The independent
Rust product and its detailed contracts already exist. Use the
[current roadmap](../roadmap/epics/index.md) for delivery and remaining work,
[ADRs](../adr/README.md) for architecture and [contracts](../contracts/README.md)
for testable behavior. Later accepted amendments, including Brain-managed skills/guidance and plugin-managed
capture/recall and the current development posture, govern their explicit changes.

Recollect owns canonical identities, source evidence, claims, correction,
permissions and execution. Collections/areas/environments organize overlapping
views inside a Brain; tool-profile grants separately authorize execution.
Local/shared installation, optional OIDC/Vault and policy-controlled automatic
learning are part of the baseline. Cognee, Atlas and claude-mem are independent
research references, not required product runtimes.

The foundations' seven capabilities remain obligations for every producer and
consumer. Delivery is established by the owning contracts, packs and scoped
proof, not by an architectural statement alone. Preserve the implemented
baseline while completing the current graph/privacy/organization acceptance.
Original foundation research is retained at the
[fixed Git checkpoint](https://github.com/MikeK184/Recollect/blob/18a8ba7ee8399f143b6e22d16c71c81b2a608ae0/docs/mappings/atlas-foundation-decisions-2026-09-13.md).
