## Failures addressed and limits

An early browser wait used an asynchronous predicate with an API that treated its
Promise as truthy; switching to official asynchronous `expect.poll` fixed the test.
Controlled tests exposed a trusted SQL finish-code allowlist missing new transient
error labels and an old uniqueness constraint that prevented policy catch-up/retries;
both were corrected before the final 33-scenario run. Reconciliation validation was
tightened to enumerated offered revision IDs and normalized redundant replace/retire
decisions after one real attempt failed with `invalid_input`. No raw response was
retained, so its precise payload defect is not claimed as known.

Three disposable databases from failed development checks were removed only after
their exact fixture ownership comments were verified. A subsequent query confirmed
none of those three remain. Passing fixtures and their restore copies cleaned up.

Reconciliation is bounded to compatible same-source-lineage candidates. Unsupported
cross-source semantic conflicts may stay uncertain. Initial handover selection is
explicit; automatic session-derived handovers belong to the later producer slices.
Cross-environment/nested handovers, conflicting spans of the same evidence ID,
automatic procedure execution and adaptive retrieval-strength scoring are outside
the accepted initial contract. No external deployment or universal model-quality
guarantee follows from this proof. Changes remain uncommitted; release version N/A.
