"Settings", tiny subtitle "Keep this Brain working the way you want.", tabs General, Access, Privacy selected, AI permissions.
One single bordered card with a header "Privacy" and small sage "Automatic" badge, top-right Cancel and sage "Save changes" controls. One form only; all sections separated by modest serif headings and hairlines, not separate cards. Thin subtle horizontal lines between EVERY editable row so each label maps clearly to its right-aligned control. Balanced column width; controls close enough to labels to follow the row. A very faint sage highlighted focused row "Raw sessions" and its focused input illustrate clear editing. No excluded-tools or excluded-content controls, no manual redaction fields, no AI transmission footer, no explanatory paragraphs. Keep all exact sections/fields:

Activity & backup: Activity detail [365 days], Managed backup window [7 days].
All text readable and aligned; full form visible in a tall desktop composition around 1680x1500. Clean 8px card radius, inputs compact with visible border, sage switches. Minimal precise UI, matching the attached reference's typographic elegance.

Do not add invented permissions, numeric counters or unsupported controls.
Main second card "Environments" with plus "Add environment" button aligned to title. A beautiful compact list of two rows: thin outlined branch icon on sage square + Development + pencil rename icon at right; thin outlined branch icon on sage square + Production + pencil rename icon. Delicate row separators. One restrained hint "Optional scopes for this Brain." No random descriptive paragraphs, no status badges or permission grants attached to environments.
